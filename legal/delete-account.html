Delete your account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Smart Transcriber and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.

This works for both email accounts and guest accounts. Guest accounts have no email address, so the app is the only way to delete them.

Can't open the app?

Email andyr@consorttech.com from the email address used for your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm your request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and account details.
  • Every recording you uploaded or recorded, including the stored audio files.
  • Transcripts, notes, AI Summaries, translations and feedback.
  • Folders and session details such as titles and descriptions.
  • Usage records linked to your account.

What may remain

  • Encrypted backups kept by our database provider can hold deleted data for a limited time until they are overwritten. We do not restore deleted accounts from them.
  • A one-way hashed device identifier used to count free usage may be kept so free limits cannot be reset by deleting and re-creating an account. It cannot be turned back into your device identifier.
  • Records we must keep to meet legal obligations, if any.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions to stop future charges.
