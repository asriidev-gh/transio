Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what Smart Transcriber collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as your email address and sign-in identifiers. Guest accounts have no email address.
  • Content you create: audio recordings, transcripts, notes, AI Summaries, translations, and related details such as titles, folders and timestamps.
  • Voice translate clips and text you submit when you use hold-to-talk translation. These are short audio clips sent for speech recognition and translation.
  • Voice translate conversation history, which is stored on your phone only.
  • A one-way hashed identifier of your Android device. We use it to count free usage and to prevent abuse. We do not store the raw device identifier.
  • Usage counts per feature, and basic technical details needed to run the service.
  • Support messages you send to us.

2. How we use information

  • To provide transcription, notes, AI Summaries, translation, Voice translate and sync across your signed-in devices.
  • To apply usage limits, secure accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support requests and fix bugs.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Services that process your data

We send your content to the following providers only to deliver features you use. We do not sell your content.

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and storage of your recordings and text.
  • Render: hosting of our server.
  • Anthropic: AI Summaries, translation and answers to your questions about a session.
  • Deepgram: speech recognition for live captions and, when enabled, uploaded audio.
  • A Whisper-compatible speech recognition provider (OpenAI by default): transcription of uploaded audio and Voice translate clips.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Spoken playback of a translation uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech, not a cloud voice service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with the providers above so the app works, when the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and content while your account is active. You can delete individual sessions in the app at any time.

You can delete your whole account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your login, recordings, transcripts, summaries, folders and usage records. Encrypted backups kept by our database provider may hold the data for a limited time until they are overwritten.

Voice translate history is stored on your phone. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Use a strong password and keep your device updated.

7. Your choices

You can delete sessions, delete your account, manage Voice translate history on your device, and control microphone and notification permissions in your phone settings. You can also contact us to ask about the data we hold for you.

8. Children

Smart Transcriber is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your region. Do not use the service if you are under that age.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, give notice in the app when practical.

10. Contact
